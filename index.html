🎧 What Is spotify-tui?

spotify-tui is a music player that runs in a text-based window instead of a large graphical app. Instead of clicking through crowded menus, you see a clean list of your music and control everything with a few keys. The project is built with HTML and a little bit of Rust, and it is meant to be used with your Spotify account on a Windows computer.

Because it is text-based, it feels light and fast. It does not take over your screen, and it does not need a lot of room. If you like keeping things simple and you enjoy having your music close at hand while you work, browse, or type, this is a comfortable way to do it.

You do not need to know anything about programming to use it. You only need to download it, open it, and sign in with your Spotify account the first time you use it.

✨ What You Can Do With It

Everything below is designed to be used with simple key presses. No mouse menus, no complicated settings screens.

  • Browse your music library: see your saved songs, albums, and artists in one tidy list.
  • Play and pause instantly: start or stop your music without leaving the window you are working in.
  • Skip and rewind: move to the next track or start the current song over whenever you want.
  • Control the volume: turn your music up or down without hunting for a slider.
  • Search for songs: type a name and find the track or artist you are in the mood for.
  • Follow your playlists: open your playlists and play them from start to finish.
  • See what is playing: the current song and artist stay visible while you work.
  • Stay light on your computer: a text-based window uses far less screen space than a full app.
Because it works alongside your existing Spotify account, your music, playlists, and saved songs are already there the first time you open it.

⌨️ Everyday Use

The whole app is built around a handful of keys. You do not need to memorise a long list. These are the ones you will use most:

  • Arrow keys: move up and down through your lists of songs, albums, and playlists.
  • Enter: select the item you have highlighted.
  • Space: play or pause the current song.
  • Right and left arrows: skip forward to the next track or go back to the previous one.
  • Up and down on the volume area: raise or lower the volume.
  • Slash or the search key: open the search box and type what you want to hear.
  • Escape: close a menu or go back one step.
  • Q: quit the app when you are finished.

If you ever forget a key, look for the small hint bar at the bottom of the window. It always shows the most useful shortcuts for wherever you are.

🛠️ If Something Does Not Work

Most small problems have a simple fix. Try these in order:

  1. Close it and open it again. This clears up most temporary hiccups.
  2. Check your internet connection. Music streaming needs a working connection, just like the Spotify website.
  3. Sign in again. If your music does not load, disconnect your Spotify account inside the app and connect it once more.
  4. Download a fresh copy. Return to the download page and get the newest version. Older versions can stop working after updates.
  5. Make sure your Spotify account is active. A free account works for many features, and a premium account unlocks playback control.
  6. Restart your computer. It sounds obvious, but it resolves a surprising number of issues.
If a button press does nothing, click once on the app window first. The window needs to be the active one before it will respond to your keys.
